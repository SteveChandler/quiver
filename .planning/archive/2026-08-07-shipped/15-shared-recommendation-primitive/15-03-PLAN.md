| Multiple beaches -> ranked region output | Tie-breaking and grouping must not be order-dependent. |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-15-07 | Denial of Service | Helper runtime | mitigate | Consume supplied rows only, limit recommendations to 3, and avoid network/database fetches. |

</threat_model>
