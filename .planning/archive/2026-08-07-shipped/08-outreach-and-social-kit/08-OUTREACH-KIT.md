# Phase 8 Warm Outreach Kit

## Boundary

These are drafts only. Do not send, paste, schedule, DM, post, or update any tracker until the user approves the exact batch.

## Warm Email Drafts

### 1. Session Logger Follow-Up

**Use for:** users with at least one logged or rated session.
**Job:** get one concrete signal about whether the log captured what mattered.
**CTA:** reply with one thing Quiver should remember next time.

**Subject options**

- What made that session work?
- Did Quiver capture the right stuff?
- Quick session question

**Preview**

I want to know whether the log captured the real reason that session worked.

```text
Hey {{first_name}},

Steven from Quiver.

I saw that you logged a session, so I wanted to ask while the details are still useful.

Did Quiver capture the stuff that actually mattered about that surf?

Not just where and when. The useful part is why it was worth it or why it was not: tide, wind, swell direction, board choice, crowd, timing, or anything else you noticed.

If there is one thing Quiver should remember before making the next call for you, reply with that.

Thanks,
Steven
```

### 2. First Log Nudge

**Use for:** native users with home beach/activity but no session log.
**Job:** identify friction before first log.
**CTA:** reply with where logging lost them.

**Subject options**

- What stopped the first log?
- Did the session log make sense?
- Quick Quiver question

**Preview**

You checked the app, but I do not think you logged a session yet.

```text
Hey {{first_name}},

Steven from Quiver.

I saw that you got into the app, but I do not think you have logged a surf session yet.

That is the part I am tightening right now.

Quiver gets more useful when it has real sessions to compare against: beach, tide, swell, wind, board, and whether the day was actually worth paddling out for you.

If you did not log one because something felt confusing, slow, annoying, or not worth the effort, can you reply and tell me where it lost you?

One sentence is enough.

Thanks,
Steven
```

### 3. Beginner / Newer Surfer Clarity Check

**Use for:** beginner or surf-camp users, web-active users without session logs, high-intent learners.
**Job:** learn whether Quiver explains enough without assuming too much.
**CTA:** reply with the confusing part.

**Subject options**

- Does Quiver make sense for beginners?
- Is the forecast clear enough?
- Quick beginner-surfer question

**Preview**

I want to know whether Quiver helps newer surfers or assumes too much.

```text
Hey {{first_name}},

I have been thinking about the beginner-surfer version of Quiver.

The app is built around explaining the forecast, not just labeling it "fair," "good," or "ok."

But I want to make sure it does not assume too much.

If you were using Quiver before a lesson, surf camp, or first few sessions on your own, what would it need to explain more clearly before you trusted the call?

Could be tide, wind, wave height, beach choice, timing, or just plain language around whether it is worth going.

One honest answer would help a lot.

Thanks,
Steven
```

## Reply Follow-Ups

### If They Ask About Price

```text
I am keeping public pricing off the site until checkout and entitlement sync are verified end to end.

Right now the honest path is the founding access waitlist. No one from beta/current usage gets charged automatically.

If you want, join the waitlist and I will send details when the release gate is actually clear.
```

### If They Ask Whether Quiver Is Better Than Surfline

```text
Different job.

Surfline is genuinely good at cams and editorial forecasting. Quiver is for the surfer who wants the call explained, tied to their beaches and sessions, and improved with feedback over time.

I am not claiming perfect accuracy. I am trying to make the reasoning visible and the feedback loop useful.
```

### If They Ask What To Do First

```text
Pick the beach you actually surf most.

Check the call before you go.

After the session, log whether it was worth it and what stood out.

That gives Quiver the signal it needs to become more useful for you.
```
