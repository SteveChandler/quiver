# Phase 8 Social Kit

## Boundary

Drafts only. Do not post, schedule, boost, DM, or hand off to a social tool until the user approves the exact copy and asset.

## Reusable Captions

### 1. Founder Problem

```text
"Fair" is not a surf forecast.

It is a verdict without enough reasoning.

Quiver is built around the call I wanted before driving to the beach: what the swell, wind, tide, and local setup mean, and whether this looks like the kind of day I actually score.
```

### 2. Learning Loop

```text
The Quiver loop is simple:

forecast -> check the beach -> log the session -> tune the next call.

The app gets more useful when surfers close the loop instead of just checking a number and disappearing.
```

### 3. Session Logger

```text
Your best sessions have fingerprints.

Tide stage. Swell direction. Wind window. Board choice. Beach. Crowd. What you would paddle out for again.

Quiver treats the log as signal for the next forecast, not a diary buried off to the side.
```

### 4. App Store Status

```text
Quiver for iPhone is on the App Store.

Open the listing, check your home break, and tell me where the first run feels unclear.

The useful feedback is specific: the beach, the call, and what the water actually looked like.
```

### 5. Founding Access Waitlist

```text
Founding access is waitlist-only while checkout is verified.

No automatic charges for beta/current users. No public price promises until the release gate is real.

If you want first access when plans open, the waitlist is live at /pricing.
```

### 6. Beginner Angle

```text
Quiver should help newer surfers understand the call, not just stare at swell charts.

If the best answer is "not today," the app should say why.

If a small clean window is actually useful for a beginner, it should explain that too.
```

### 7. Honest Limits

```text
Quiver is not finished.

Forecasts can miss. Coverage can have gaps. The personal layer only gets sharper when surfers log what actually happened.

That is why I am keeping this launch focused on real feedback, not hype.
```

### 8. Warm Ask

```text
If you use Quiver this week, send me the miss.

The beach.
The forecast.
What you saw in the water.
Whether you would have paddled out again.

That is the signal that makes the product better.
```

### 9. Forecast Timing

```text
A forecast should tell you when, not just if.
```

### 10. Different Surfers, Different Calls

```text
Paddle or coffee?

Same conditions, different surfer, different answer.

Good depends on who is asking.
```

### 11. Beach-First Trust

```text
Built for surfers who check the tide before coffee and still trust their own read.
```

### 12. Founding Feedback

```text
Founding access is for honest feedback:

log a session,
tell us where the call felt wrong,
help tune the next one.
```

## Short Video / Reel Scripts

### Script A: One Surf Call

**Length:** 18-22 seconds.
**Asset bias:** launch hero render + app preview.

1. Ocean/product open.
   - On-screen: `Stop translating five forecasts.`
2. App forecast screen.
   - On-screen: `Quiver gives one call with the reasoning.`
3. Session log screen.
   - On-screen: `Then your session teaches the next one.`
4. App Store / logo close.
   - On-screen: `Open Quiver on the App Store.`

**Voiceover option**

```text
Most surf apps hand you a verdict. Quiver shows the call, lets you check it against the beach, and learns when you log the session after.
```

### Script B: The Session Memory

**Length:** 20-25 seconds.
**Asset bias:** session-log screenshot, discovery/similarity screenshot, app icon.

1. Session log UI.
   - On-screen: `The log is not the feature.`
2. Board/tide/swell/rating details.
   - On-screen: `It is the signal.`
3. Forecast/match UI.
   - On-screen: `Quiver compares new windows to days you scored.`
4. Close.
   - On-screen: `Forecast. Check. Log. Adjust.`

**Voiceover option**

```text
Your best days have a pattern. Quiver is built to remember that pattern and make the next call more useful.
```

### Script C: Honest Founder Check

**Length:** 25-30 seconds.
**Asset bias:** founder-style screen recording, not polished ad only.

1. Founder screen recording begins on home/forecast.
   - On-screen: `What I am watching before launch.`
2. Tap through forecast reasoning.
   - On-screen: `Does the call explain itself?`
3. Tap session log.
   - On-screen: `Does logging feel worth it?`
4. Tap pricing/waitlist.
   - On-screen: `Founding access stays waitlist-only until checkout is verified.`
5. Close.
   - On-screen: `Send me the miss.`

**Voiceover option**

```text
I do not need polished praise right now. I need to know where the call breaks, where the log feels slow, and what Quiver should remember next time.
```

### Script D: Forecast Report Card

**Length:** 18-22 seconds.
**Asset bias:** La Jolla report-card video, Quiver forecast receipt, beach-check receipt, later clean-wave proof.

1. Early forecast receipt.
   - On-screen: `The forecast said fair.`
2. Beach check.
   - On-screen: `Quiver liked the later window.`
3. Later waves.
   - On-screen: `So we checked both.`
4. Close.
   - On-screen: `A forecast should tell you when, not just if.`

**CTA option**

```text
Would you have waited?
```

### Script E: Same Beach, Three Surfers

**Length:** 15-20 seconds.
**Asset bias:** same beach conditions plus text-call overlays.

1. Conditions open.
   - On-screen: `Same beach. Three different calls.`
2. Beginner overlay.
   - On-screen: `Foamie only, or coffee.`
3. Longboarder overlay.
   - On-screen: `Worth a paddle.`
4. Shortboarder overlay.
   - On-screen: `Wait.`
5. Close.
   - On-screen: `Good depends on who is asking.`

**CTA option**

```text
Paddle or pass?
```

## Copy Guardrails

- Say `Open App Store` for current iPhone CTAs until Apple status is rechecked.
- Say `founding access waitlist`, not `buy lifetime`, `claim lifetime`, or `lock in`.
- Say `learns from sessions` or `remembers your sessions`, not `AI-powered`.
- Say `shows the reasoning`, not `more accurate than Surfline`.
- Keep Reddit social-public copy educational and comment-first; do not use these captions as Reddit launch posts.
- Treat older Twitter/playbook drafts as stale wherever they conflict with current iOS preorder, waitlist-only pricing, ICP, Reddit, Instagram, or TikTok guidance.
