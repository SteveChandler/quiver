<threat_model>
## Threat Model

- Risk: positive-lift claims are made from sparse, stale, or negative metrics.
  Mitigation: centralize `canClaimImprovement` and test each failure mode.
- Risk: a missing service-role key crashes or blanks a public route. Mitigation:
  preserve graceful client creation fallback and return a building report.
- Risk: execution invents fallback beach metrics. Mitigation: fallback rows must
  be status rows, not fake beach-level MAE rows.
</threat_model>
