# Plan 04-01: Pricing Route And Component Shape

## Goal

Choose the route and component shape for a public pricing surface that follows existing App Router and landing-page patterns while respecting Phase 2 release gates.

## Scope

- Inspect `app/`, `components/`, sitemap, landing composition, metadata helpers, and Phase 2 pricing gates.
- Add a dedicated `/pricing` route rather than overloading the landing page.
- Keep the page server-first, with a small client CTA only where auth modal interaction is required.
- Add a pricing component directory with local architecture notes.

## Non-Goals

- Do not add checkout.
- Do not add prices or plan purchase claims.
- Do not change entitlement, RevenueCat, App Store, or production database behavior.

## Verification

- Route renders in local browser.
- Component shape is covered by targeted unit tests in later Phase 4 plans.
