## Threat Model

- Risk: a shared helper accidentally enables Session Intelligence across every spot or utility page. Mitigation: allowlist exact routes and assert negative examples.
- Risk: utility pages claim unavailable buoy, cam, tide, or user-report sources. Mitigation: classify every surface with supported source hints and handoff/full-window capability.
- Risk: SEO pages are retargeted through copy or canonical drift. Mitigation: guardrail doc states frozen route/canonical behavior and later plans verify it.
</threat_model>
