## Final decisions (binding for 20.1-03..06)

- **Follows ≠ favorites.** New additive `beach_follows` table (RLS owner-only, unique
  (user_id,beach_id), bounded topic CHECK, touch trigger, rollback notes) — **not applied**;
  production application is approval-gated. Rationale: free-tier favorites cap (3) would make
  anonymous merge lossy/entitlement-changing, and `favorite_beaches` feeds alert rules and
  cron/email audiences.
- **Merge policy:** tombstones — explicit anonymous removals apply to server state once on merge,
  then clear; unions are deduped by (beachId, topic); repeat merges are no-ops.
  `MAX_FOLLOWED_BEACHES = 50`; topics enum: surf, water_temp, tide, water_quality, wind, general.
- **Intent (D-11..D-14):** pure classifier; explicit > inferred; no evidence → unknown; one
  utility view never qualifies; inferred surfing needs ≥2 surf signals or 1 high-intent action.
- **Handoff:** versioned expiring `HandoffContext` (bounded enums/ids, no tokens/PII) with
  `exact | replaced | beach_only | invalid` resolution classification; extends the audited
  `app/app/spot` + `forecast-window-share` path.
- **Watched call:** identity composes existing `AlertRule` + `RecommendationSessionContext`
  (Pick), never a second forecast record. Updates are a discriminated union
  (`still_on`/`call_changed`/`better_nearby`) with cause narrowing, dedupe keys, suppression that
  keeps the watch active, exact-reopen resolution, and manual-reopen sources.
- **Analytics:** 9 web + 11 native BFR events; anonymous events join the signed-out allowlist;
  `BFR_TOPICS` derives from `FollowTopic`; `home_mode` mirrors canonical `now/best/my-spots`;
  new web events carry ZERO implicit-preference weight (web intent cannot pollute native surf
  preferences); analytics failures cannot throw into product actions; session events untouched.
  `bfr-follow-holdout-v1` deterministic assignment for BFR-12.
