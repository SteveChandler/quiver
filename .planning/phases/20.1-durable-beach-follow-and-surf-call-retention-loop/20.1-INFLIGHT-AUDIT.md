- TD-04 — remove retired forecast prototype;
- TD-05 — retire dormant Plan My Next Session policy stack.

## Classification rules

- **Active execution:** open PR, current worktree, or documented uncommitted implementation.
- **Release verification:** merged code whose migration, OTA/binary, device, flag, or telemetry proof remains open.
- **Parallel backlog:** open issue without current Phase 20.1 ownership.
- **Branch residue:** branch content already merged/superseded; name alone is not active work.
- **Unresolved unique branch:** unique commits without a current PR/owner disposition.
- **Existing baseline:** shipped behavior to preserve, not rebuild as the phase lever.
- **Cleanup candidate:** deletion proposed by the local audit that requires exact current-main reference and live-owner proof.
