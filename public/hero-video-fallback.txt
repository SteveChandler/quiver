Hero Video Setup Instructions
=============================

The landing page is configured to display a background video with the following specifications:

1. File Location: /public/hero-surf-video.mp4
2. Video should be:
   - High quality surf footage (1920x1080 or higher)
   - 10-30 seconds long for optimal loading
   - Optimized for web (H.264 codec recommended)
   - Looping seamlessly

3. Fallback: If no video is provided, the component will fall back to the placeholder.jpg image

Recommended Sources for Stock Surf Videos:
- Pexels (free): https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/surfing/
- Unsplash (free): https://unsplash.com/s/videos/surfing
- Adobe Stock (paid): Search for "surfing ocean waves"

The video should showcase beautiful surf conditions to immediately capture the user's attention and convey the app's purpose.
