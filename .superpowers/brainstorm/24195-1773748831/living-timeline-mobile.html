How this works on mobile

  • Swipe the ocean: Thumb drags horizontally through time. Wave surface height, wind particles, and golden glow all shift as you scrub.
  • Golden windows glow: The optimal surf windows have a warm amber glow in the ocean canvas — you see them before reading any numbers.
  • Waves animate at real period: If swell period is 14s, wave pulses come every ~14s. Faster period = choppier animation. You feel the conditions.
  • Tap the golden zone: Tapping within a golden window opens the session briefing card with eerily specific predictions.
  • The briefing card is share-ready: Screenshot it, send to the group chat. "Be at Blacks at 6:47am."
  • Tide curve stays visible: Always shows the tidal context beneath the ocean — no need to switch tabs.
  • Performance: CSS animations for waves + wind (no canvas/WebGL needed). Degrades to static on reduced-motion.
