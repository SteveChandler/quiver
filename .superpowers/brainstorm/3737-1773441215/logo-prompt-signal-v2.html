Logo Prompt v2 — "The Signal" (Fused)

Rewritten to force true fusion — one line where you can't separate the wave from the data.

Primary Prompt — Fused

Gemini Prompt — Copy/Paste Ready
Design a single abstract logo mark for a brand called "Quiver." The entire mark is one continuous bold stroke — a single unbroken line that IS both a wave and a signal simultaneously. Not a wave on one side transitioning into a signal on the other — the whole line must be ambiguous at every point along its path. You should not be able to point to any section and say "that part is the wave" or "that part is the data." The key: imagine a swell forecast graph where the data visualization itself has the fluid, cresting, organic energy of the ocean it measures. The line rises and falls with the rhythm of both a heartbeat monitor and a rolling swell. Every peak could be a wave crest or a data spike. Every trough could be a wave valley or a signal dip. The form is indivisible — one thing, not two things combined. The stroke should have confident thick-to-thin calligraphic variation — thickening at peaks, thinning at transitions — which gives it both the feeling of a hand-drawn brushstroke and a rendered data line with weighted emphasis. Style: Clean flat vector logo. One continuous bold stroke with calligraphic weight variation. No fills, no enclosed shapes, no gradients, no shading. The mark is only the line. Color: Deep navy (#252D6B) on white background. Single color. Must work as a solid silhouette. Composition: Centered with generous whitespace. Compact — roughly 3:1 or 2:1 horizontal proportion. Must read clearly when scaled down to 32 pixels. No text, no wordmark — only the stroke. Critical: Do NOT create a wave shape on the left that transitions into a signal shape on the right. Do NOT create two visual ideas joined together. The entire stroke must be one unified rhythm that is simultaneously oceanic and data-like at every point. Think of it as a new kind of line that has never existed — not a wave, not a graph, but a third thing that contains both.

If Gemini Still Splits Them

Follow-up refinement prompts to push harder on fusion

Refinement — Push Toward Ambiguity
This is still reading as two separate ideas stitched together. I need the entire mark to be ambiguous — every segment of the line should make someone ask "is that a wave or a data point?" Simplify: use only 3-4 peaks and valleys total. Make the rhythm even and consistent across the whole stroke. No section should look more "oceanic" or more "digital" than any other section. One unified pulse.
Refinement — Simplify Further
Simpler. Reduce to just 2-3 flowing peaks. Think of the simplest possible line that still carries rhythm and movement. Like a Nike swoosh that undulates — bold, immediate, one read. The fewer curves, the more iconic and the better it works at small sizes.

Square Icon Variant

Gemini Prompt — App Icon Version
Take the same continuous stroke concept, but make the path curve and fold to fit within a square proportion. The line can loop or double back on itself while remaining one continuous open stroke. It should feel coiled with energy — a signal compressed into a compact form. Deep navy (#252D6B) on white, flat vector, no text. Must be instantly recognizable at 32x32 pixels.

What Changed From v1

  • Explicit anti-pattern: "Do NOT create a wave on the left that transitions into a signal on the right" — directly addresses the problem Gemini produced
  • Fusion language throughout: "simultaneously," "indivisible," "at every point along its path" — no room for interpretation as two concepts
  • Added refinement prompts: Because Gemini may still default to the split — these give you escalation language
  • Added square icon variant: Separate prompt for app icon proportions instead of hoping the horizontal mark fits
