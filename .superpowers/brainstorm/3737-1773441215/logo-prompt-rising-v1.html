Logo Prompt — "The Rise"

An ascending organic form, permanently frozen in the moment before the peak.

Primary Prompt

Gemini Prompt — Copy/Paste Ready
Design a single abstract logo mark for a brand called "Quiver." The mark should be an organic, ascending form — a smooth, fluid shape that is clearly rising, building momentum, and frozen in the instant before it peaks. It should feel like potential energy made visible: something powerful is about to happen but hasn't yet. The shape should be abstract — not a wave, not a flame, not an arrow. It is its own thing: a rising form with smooth flowing curves that accelerates upward. Think of the feeling when you're sitting in the ocean and the water lifts beneath you as a set approaches — that silent, building energy translated into a single compact shape. The form should feel grown, not built. Organic curves, no sharp angles, no straight lines. It should have a sense of weight at the base that becomes lighter and more dynamic toward the top — grounded but reaching. One unified solid shape, not a line or stroke. Style: Clean flat vector logo. A single solid filled shape. No gradients, no shading, no texture, no outlines. Pure silhouette that works at any size. Color: Deep twilight navy (#252D6B) on a clean white background. Single color only. Composition: Centered on white with generous whitespace. The mark should be vertically oriented or nearly square — taller than wide, reflecting its upward energy. Must read clearly and feel iconic at 32x32 pixels. No text, no wordmark, no tagline — just the shape. Do not include: literal waves, water, surfboards, flames, arrows, feathers, leaves, birds, mountains, letter forms, gradients, shadows, 3D effects, multiple disconnected elements, or any text.

Variation Prompts

Variation A — More Minimal
Same concept, but radically simpler. Reduce the form to the absolute minimum number of curves needed to convey "rising energy." Think of what remains if you stripped away everything except the essential gesture. Two or three curves at most. The simplest possible shape that still feels like it's ascending and alive.
Variation B — Asymmetric
Same concept, but the form should be deliberately asymmetric — heavier on one side, leaning into its momentum. Not balanced or centered, but dynamic and off-kilter like something caught mid-movement. The asymmetry should make it feel alive and directional, as if it would tip over if the energy released.
Variation C — Negative Space
Same rising form concept, but use negative space within the shape to create a sense of internal energy or tension. A channel, gap, or void cut through the solid form — as if the energy building inside is about to split the shape open. The negative space should feel purposeful, not decorative. Still one shape, but with a meaningful absence inside it.

Iteration Strategy

  • Generate 4+ with the primary prompt first — see where Gemini's instincts go with "rising organic form"
  • Watch for common traps: Gemini may produce flames, leaves, or feathers — these are "rising organic shapes" it knows. The exclusion list should catch them, but if they slip through, add "This is not a natural object. It is a pure abstract form."
  • Favorite → refine: "Keep this exact shape but make it [simpler / more asymmetric / more compact / heavier at the base]"
  • 32px test: Shrink every candidate. Rising forms tend to scale well because the silhouette is distinctive.
  • Orange accent: Once you have a navy shape you love — "Same mark, but the upper portion where the energy is most intense transitions to charming orange (#F78E42)"
  • Wordmark: Handle separately. A rising mark pairs well with "Quiver" set to the right in Space Grotesk.
