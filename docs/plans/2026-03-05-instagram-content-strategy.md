## Target Audience

### Primary: Beginner & Intermediate Surfers

- **4.2 million surfers** in the U.S. (2024, SIMA)
- **35-40% started in the last 5 years** — roughly 1.5-1.7 million newer surfers

**Average beginner profile:** ~34 years old, $75K household income, college-educated, discovered surfing through Instagram/TikTok or a friend, spends ~$1,200/year on surf-related products and services.

## Beginner Surfer Workflow (Pain Points = Content Opportunities)

Research from forums, Reddit, surf schools, and coaching blogs reveals a consistent decision-making flow:

### The Typical Beginner Decision Flow

1. **Trigger** — Friend texts, weekend arrives, or they see surf content on IG/TikTok. Starts with a feeling, not data.
2. **Check an app** — Look at the color rating as a binary go/no-go. Problem: most apps rate for experienced surfers. A "Poor" day is often perfect for beginners.
3. **Check the live cam** — Gut-check what the forecast says. Most trusted single data point.
4. **Ask someone** — Text a friend, ask a shop employee, post online with a screenshot: "Is this good for a beginner?"
5. **Drive to the beach and observe** — Look for other beginners, foam boards, mellow vibes before committing.
6. **Get in (or bail)** — Intimidation, crowds, and etiquette anxiety cause many to leave without paddling out.

### Key Pain Points

| Pain Point | Content Opportunity |
|---|---|
| App ratings are inverted for beginners — "Poor" = great beginner day | Teach beginners to see small/clean days as wins |
| Swell height vs wave face height confusion | "What 2ft actually looks like" visual explainers |
| Tide advice is always "it depends" | Spot-specific tide guides for beginner spots |
| Information overload — too many numbers | "The only 3 numbers you need" simplified forecast breakdowns |
| Social intimidation / etiquette fear | "Beginner etiquette" and "spots where beginners are welcome" guides |
| Forecast accuracy distrust after being burned | "How to cross-check your forecast" trust-building content |
| Wind direction terminology confusion | Simple offshore vs onshore visual explainers |

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: "Should I Go?" — Your Surf Buddy's Weekend Call

Quiver is the friend who texts you "Saturday morning is the move."

**Recurring post types:**

- **"Weekend Wave Check"** — Every Thursday/Friday, a clean branded graphic: which day is best for beginners this weekend in San Diego, and why, in plain English. "Small and clean Saturday AM — perfect for practicing pop-ups. Sunday gets choppy by noon."
- **"Today's a Go / Today's a No"** — Simple daily story format. Green = get out there. Orange = maybe if you're comfortable. Red = rest day. With a one-line explanation.
- **"3 Numbers, That's It"** — Carousel series teaching beginners what to actually look at in a forecast. Branded as "the Quiver way to read waves."

**Voice:** "We checked so you don't have to. Here's the deal."

---

### Pillar 2: "Where Should I Go?" — Spot Guides by Surfers, for Beginners

Curated recommendations from the community perspective. Solves the "arrive, observe, decide" step before they leave the house.

**Recurring post types:**

- **"Beginner Spot Check"** — Single spot breakdowns: best tide window, what conditions work, crowd vibe, parking, and honestly — is this spot welcoming to learners? One per week, builds a library.
- **"Conditions like today? Try these spots."** — Match today's actual forecast to 2-3 SD spots that'll work for beginners right now. Practical, timely, shareable.
- **"Longboard Sundays"** — Weekly post for the longboard niche. Small wave spots that are paradise on a log. Celebrates the slow, stylish side of surfing.
- **"What to look for from the parking lot"** — Teach the observation skills: foam boards in the water = friendly lineup, how to spot rips, reading the crowd before you commit.

**Voice:** "We surf these spots. Here's what we'd tell a friend."

---

### Pillar 3: "Learn the Ocean" — Bite-Sized Surf Education

Positions Quiver as the buddy who teaches you stuff without making you feel dumb. All producible solo with Quiver data + basic graphics.

**Recurring post types:**

- **"WTF is swell period?"** — Single-concept explainers. One post, one topic. Swell period. Offshore vs onshore. What "closeout" means. Why low tide changes your spot. Simple graphics, plain language.
- **"Surf terms, no BS"** — Carousel series: the term, what it actually means, and why you should care as a beginner. Build a glossary over time.
- **"Tide Talk"** — "Here's what high tide does to [specific SD spot]" with before/after style visuals or Quiver screenshots showing the difference.
- **"Read the beach"** — Teach observation skills: where the rip is, where the sandbar is, why everyone's sitting in that one spot. Stuff you learn after years of surfing, handed to beginners for free.

**Voice:** "Nobody taught us this either. Here's what we wish we knew."

## Posting Cadence

### Weekly Schedule

| Format | Frequency | Day/Time | Pillar |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Weekend Wave Check** (feed post) | 1x/week | Thursday 5-6 PM | Should I Go? |
| **Spot Check** (carousel) | 1x/week | Tuesday 11 AM-12 PM | Where Should I Go? |
| **Learn the Ocean** (carousel) | 1x/week | Sunday 10 AM | Learn the Ocean |
| **Today's a Go/No** (story) | 3-5x/week | Morning 6-7 AM | Should I Go? |
| **Longboard Sundays** (feed post) | 1x/week | Sunday 4 PM | Where Should I Go? |

**Total: 4 feed posts/week + daily-ish stories.** Sustainable for one person if you batch-create carousels on one day and do stories live.

### Why These Times

- Thursday PM = people planning weekends
- Morning stories = surfers check conditions early
- Sunday = relaxed scroll time, engagement peaks
- Tuesday midday = midweek engagement sweet spot

### Threads Strategy

Not just cross-posting — Threads rewards a different format:

- **"Learn the Ocean" explainers** → Rewrite as short text threads (3-5 posts). More conversational. "Ok so swell period. Everyone talks about wave height but this is the number that actually matters. Here's why..."
- **Hot takes / opinions** → "Unpopular opinion: the best days to learn to surf are the days most apps tell you to stay home." Spark engagement.
- **"Ask me anything about SD surf"** → Open-ended question posts. Low effort, high engagement.
- **Frequency:** 3x/week. Same day as IG posts but rewritten for the format.

## Visual Style & Templates

### 3 Reusable Templates

**Template A: "Weekend Wave Check" (single graphic)**
- Clean, bold layout
- Top: "Weekend Wave Check" + Quiver logo
- Middle: Day-by-day breakdown (Sat AM / Sat PM / Sun AM / Sun PM)
- Each day gets a simple verdict: Go / Maybe / Skip
- One line of plain English per day
- Bottom: "quiversurf.app" subtle watermark
- Colors: Match Quiver's brand palette (teal/navy tones)

**Template B: "Spot Check / Explainer" (carousel)**
- Slide 1: Bold title on branded background ("WTF Is Swell Period?")
- Slides 2-5: One concept per slide, large text, minimal graphics
- Final slide: Quiver CTA — "See your forecast in plain English" + app screenshot or link
- Consistent font, colors, spacing across all carousels
- Avoid stock photo vibes — keep it graphic/typographic, not cheesy lifestyle imagery

**Template C: "Longboard Sunday / Today's a Go" (single graphic)**
- Moodier, more photo-forward if images available
- Or same graphic style as Template A with a different color accent
- Minimal text — the vibe matters more than information density

### Design Rules

- **Tool:** Canva (free tier is fine) — create 3 master templates, duplicate and swap content each week
- **Fonts:** One bold sans-serif for headlines, one clean sans for body
- **Colors:** Stick to 2-3 brand colors max (Quiver teal/navy palette)
- **Golden rule:** Every post should be recognizable as Quiver within 2 seconds of scrolling. Same colors, same font treatment, same logo placement. Consistency > creativity at this stage.

## Growth Tactics

### Hashtag Strategy

Mix of broad + niche tags per post (15-20 total). Rotate tags across posts — don't use the same set every time (IG deprioritizes this).

- **Broad:** `#surfing` `#learntosur` `#surfforecast` `#beginnersurfer`
- **Niche:** `#longboarding` `#noseriding` `#vanlifesurf` `#surfcalifornia` `#sandiegosurf`
- **SD spot-specific:** `#lajollashores` `#pacificbeach` `#tourmaline` `#cardiffreef` `#oceansidesurf` `#delmar` `#sandiegosurfing` `#socalsur`

### Week 1-4: Seed the Audience (No Personal Network)

1. **Follow and engage first.** Follow 50-100 beginner surf accounts, SD surf schools, longboard accounts, and local surf photographers. Like and leave genuine comments (not "nice!" — actual thoughts). This is your primary growth lever in month 1.
2. **DM surf schools — offer content, not promotion.** Example message:
   > "Hey! I'm building a surf forecast page for San Diego focused on beginners — breaking down conditions in plain English instead of confusing numbers. I'm putting together beginner spot guides and want to feature [their beach/area]. Would you be cool with me tagging you guys? Happy to link to your lesson page too."
   - **Target list:** Surf Diva (La Jolla Shores), Menehune Surf School, San Diego Surf School, South Coast Surf Shop, Bird Rock Surf Shop
   - Send 5-6 DMs in week 1. Even 2 responses = 2 reshares to their audience of actual SD beginners.
3. **Tag spots and local accounts** in every post. Beaches, surf shops, local photographers. Some will reshare.
4. **Cross-post to Threads.** "Learn the Ocean" explainers rewritten as conversational text threads.

### Month 2+: Scale

- **Collab posts** with surf photographers — they provide the photo, Quiver provides the forecast/conditions context. Both accounts get reach.
- **"Conditions like today" posts** are highly shareable — when someone sees conditions match their local spot, they share to story.
- **Reels (when ready)** — Canva can animate templates into slideshows with music. No video shooting needed.
- **Paid promotion ($50-100/month):** Identify 2-3 posts with highest organic reach, boost with $5-10/day for 7 days. Target: San Diego, ages 18-45, interests: surfing, longboarding, beach lifestyle. Goal: profile visits and bio link clicks.

### What NOT to Do

- Don't link to Quiver in every caption. Bio link is enough.
- Don't buy followers. Ever.
- Don't post and ghost — engagement on other accounts matters more than your own posts in month 1.
- Don't self-promote in other communities (learned from the Reddit ban).
- Don't DM people about "my app" — offer content and value first.

## Engagement Protocol

**Response time:** Within 2 hours during 6 AM - 9 PM. Faster on stories.

**DM responses:**
- Forecast questions ("Is today good for a beginner?") → Answer directly, then: "Check [spot] on Quiver for the full breakdown." This is the #1 conversion opportunity.
- Spot recommendations → Answer genuinely. Don't gate content behind the app.
- Gear questions → Answer if you can, or point to a local SD shop. Being helpful > staying on brand.

**Comment responses:**
- Reply to every comment in the first 3 months. Every single one.
- Ask follow-up questions in replies to keep threads going ("Which spot are you heading to?")

**Tone:** Same as posts — casual, helpful, no jargon. Like texting a friend.
