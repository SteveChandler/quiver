# Domain Authority Playbook: 12-Month Off-Site SEO Roadmap

**Complements:** `docs/seo/PHASE_2_SEO_TRAFFIC_PLAN.md` (on-page SEO)
**Focus:** Off-site authority building only — backlinks, referring domains, brand mentions
**Baseline:** ~8,500 indexed URLs, strong on-page SEO, zero off-site authority infrastructure (March 2026)
**Target:** 500+ referring domains and 10x+ organic traffic by March 2027

---

## The Angle

Surfline, Magicseaweed, and Windguru all publish forecasts. None of them publish accuracy data. They have every incentive to keep that information opaque — their business model depends on users trusting a black box.

Quiver is the only surf forecast that shows its work: ML accuracy vs. NOAA baseline, publicly, at the beach level. This is not a minor differentiator. It is the only honest position in the market. Every piece of outreach, every guest post pitch, every HARO response should lead with this.

The hook: "We built a surf forecast that publishes its own accuracy data, because we think surfers deserve to know if the forecast is actually working."

---

## The Backlink Engine

Two assets generate backlinks passively once they exist:

1. **The `/forecast-accuracy` page** — A public, data-driven page showing Quiver's ML accuracy vs. NOAA baseline at the beach level. Journalists, bloggers, and academics can cite it as a primary source. Build it once, keep the data updated monthly.

2. **The embed widget program** — Surf schools, coastal businesses, and tourism sites embed Quiver widgets (`tides`, `conditions`, `surf-terminal`, `ticker`). Every embed has a "Powered by Quiver" attribution link. Four widget types already exist. The `embed_impressions` table already tracks adoption. The backlink infrastructure is already built — it just needs distribution.

Both assets compound over time without ongoing effort proportional to returns.

**Template rules (2026-08-04 — these exist because the previous template broke in production):**

1. **Link the recipient's own live panel**, `https://www.quiversurf.app/embed/conditions/<beach-slug>`.
   The previous template pointed at `quiversurf.app/embed-for-surf-schools`, **a URL that never
   existed**. Roughly ten pitches went out with a 404 as their primary call to action. A 308 redirect
   now recovers them, but never link a path you have not loaded yourself.
2. **Verify the slug before sending.** `curl -sL -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}"` the exact embed URL and
   confirm `200`. A bad slug also returns a clean 404, so this check is meaningful.
3. **Include the iframe inline.** Do not close with "want me to send the snippet?" — that forces a reply
   before anyone can install, and reply rates are low.
4. **No accuracy claims and no link to `/forecast-accuracy`.** No "the only surf forecast that publishes
   accuracy", no "shows its work" superiority framing. See `Brand-Vault/marketing/icp-and-positioning.md`.
5. **No "ML-powered", "AI-powered", or "smart"** — forbidden tech-marketing tells. Say "live NOAA and
   buoy updates".
6. Snippet shape must match `components/beach-detail/embed-code-modal.tsx` (currently `height="220"`
   for conditions, `300` for tides). If that component changes, update this template.

**Follow-up sequence:**
- Day 1: Initial email (personalized to their specific beach)
- Day 5: One follow-up only ("Did you get a chance to look at the widget?")
- No further follow-up — preserve the relationship for future contact

**Tracking:**
- Log all outreach in a simple spreadsheet: School, Date Contacted, Response, Embed Confirmed, Domain
- Cross-reference against `embed_impressions` table once embeds are live: `SELECT DISTINCT domain FROM embed_impressions WHERE widget_type IS NOT NULL`
- Monthly: report new referring domains from school embeds

**Month 1-2 target:** Contact 40 surf schools; convert 8-12 to active embeds.

- Aggregate data on optimal surf windows by beach and season

### 2.3 Forecast Accuracy Report as a Citable Source

The `/forecast-accuracy` page should be designed to be cited, not just visited. This means:

- Include a canonical "How to cite this report" section with BibTeX and plain-language formats
- Add a monthly "last updated" date to signal freshness
- Include methodology notes (data sources, sample sizes, calculation methods) — academics and journalists need this to cite responsibly
- Use `schema.org/Dataset` structured data so the page appears in Google Dataset Search
- Include a direct download link for raw accuracy data in CSV format

When the page exists, proactively pitch it as a reference to:
- University surf science programs (UCSD Scripps, UCSB, University of Hawaii)
- NOAA-adjacent organizations that publish coastal data
- Citizen science aggregators (OpenMarine, iNaturalist adjacent communities)
- Wikipedia editors who maintain surf-related articles (these are perpetual citation targets)

| Thursday | Follow up on embeds confirmed but not yet live; check `embed_impressions` for new domains |
| Friday | Research next week's targets; identify 1 new link opportunity from competitor backlink analysis |

**Monthly activities:**

- Refresh the `/forecast-accuracy` page with updated data (signals freshness, keeps the page link-worthy)

- Pull referring domain count from Ahrefs Webmaster Tools; update tracking spreadsheet

### 3.3 Broken Link Building

Find pages that link to dead surf resources and offer Quiver as a replacement. This is the highest conversion rate link building tactic because you're solving a problem for the site owner.

**Targets to check for dead links:**
- Surf school "resources" or "conditions" pages that link to defunct sites

**Process:**
1. Use Ahrefs Webmaster Tools or Moz Link Explorer to find pages in the surf niche with broken outbound links

3. Email the site owner: "I noticed your link to [dead URL] on [page] is broken. Quiver covers [beach/region] and might be a good replacement."

```

Outreach Sent: [Number]

New Widget Embeds Confirmed: [Number]
embed_impressions New Domains: [Number]
```

**SQL for widget tracking (run monthly):**
```sql
SELECT
  COUNT(DISTINCT domain) AS referring_domains_from_widgets,
  COUNT(*) AS total_impressions,
  widget_type,
  DATE_TRUNC('month', created_at) AS month
FROM embed_impressions
GROUP BY widget_type, DATE_TRUNC('month', created_at)
ORDER BY month DESC, total_impressions DESC;
```

The transparency angle is not just a marketing claim — it is a structural advantage for link building. Journalists writing about surf, weather, or machine learning have a specific reason to cite Quiver that they do not have for Surfline. Surfline cannot replicate this without conceding that their current opaque approach was wrong.

Do not position Quiver as "better than Surfline at forecasting." Position it as "the forecast that shows its work." The former invites a dispute Quiver cannot definitively win. The latter is an objective, verifiable fact.
