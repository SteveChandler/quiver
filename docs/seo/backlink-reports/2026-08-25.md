### Root cause of the orphan spike — found, and it is a side effect of a correct fix

Traced during this run against the live site:

1. `eaeda9671` (18 Aug, "make the sitemap decide indexability the way the sub-pages
   do") aligned the sitemap's indexability predicate with the sub-pages' own. That
   fix is right, and the 18 Aug report asked for it. Its effect is that many
   water-temp and tide sub-pages that previously diverged now qualify and enter the
   sitemap as indexable. The 18 Aug Ahrefs crawl predates it; the 24 Aug crawl is
   the first to see the result.
2. The sitemap now carries **346 beach-level `/{state}/{city}/{beach}/water-temp`
   pages**, plus 59 city-level `/water-temp/*`, 59 `/tide/*`, and 52
   `/best-time-to-surf/*`.
3. **Nothing links to the beach-level ones.** Fetched `/ca/encinitas/swamis` live:
   41 unique internal links, and it links to `/water-temp/san-diego` and
   `/tide/san-diego` — the *city* pages — but **not** to its own
   `/ca/encinitas/swamis/water-temp`. That pattern, times 346 pages, is the orphan
   population.
4. Across six hub pages (`/`, `/beaches`, `/forecast`, `/map`, `/cams`,
   `/tools/water-quality`) there is exactly **one** link into `/water-temp/*` in
   total and **zero** into `/surf-cams/*`.
5. `/water-temp` and `/surf-cams` **both return HTTP 404**, so there is no index
   hub that could carry those links. `/water-temp/san-diego` (54 visits this week)
   and `/surf-cams/san-diego` (33 visits) are live 200s underneath a 404 parent.

These orphans are not dead weight — they are earning traffic while orphaned.
`/ca/encinitas/swamis/water-temp` took 38 visits this week and
`/nc/kill-devil-hills/.../water-temp` 23. Internal linking here is upside, not
hygiene.

| Adjusted (bot-filtered) | 5,134 | 2,074 |

| Bounce rate | 72% | 70% |
| **Singapore visits** | **4,162** | **82** |
| **US visits** | **1,306** | **2,101** |

The spike was Singapore-origin traffic — 4,162 of 5,568 views, concentrated on
`/app/handoff` (597), `/map` (348) and `/tools/water-quality` (193), all of which
have collapsed out of the top pages. The bot filter caught only 434 of it, so
"adjusted page views" was not a trustworthy series that week.

Strip it out and the underlying week is **good**: unique visitors up 754 → 839,
US visits up 1,306 → 2,101 (+61%), bounce rate down 2 points, and the top-pages
list is now dominated by real destination pages (`/water-temp/san-diego`,
`/ca/encinitas/swamis/water-temp`, `/ca/san-diego/pacific-beach`,
`/surf-cams/san-diego`) instead of one handoff route. Mobile share rose from 11%
to 40% of visits, which is what a real-user week looks like.
