Writing this page would publish a capability claim the product does not support,
which is the failure `docs/seo/`-adjacent work already hit once on

## Content opportunities (GSC-sourced, outside the query set)

The 30-query set is a pinned measurement instrument and is deliberately blind to
demand it does not ask about. Every content action above is `[NO ACTION]`
because the queries *in the set* are ranking problems on pages that already
exist. That is a correct finding about the set, not a finding that no content is
worth writing. The following come from `Brand-Vault/seo-audit/2026-08-24/GSC-EXPORT.json`
(28d window 2026-07-25 → 2026-08-21) and are **not** derived from the AEO proxy.
They are listed separately so a future reader does not attribute them to it.

### 1. City-level water-temperature pages do not exist (0 of 406)

All 338 water-temp pages in the sitemap are **beach-level**
(`/{state}/{city}/{beach}/water-temp`). There are **zero** city-level pages. But
the top-25 GSC queries are overwhelmingly **city-level**: `santa cruz water temp`
(717 imp), `santa monica water temperature` (535), `encinitas water temp` (349),
`ogunquit beach water temperature` (295). Santa Cruz has five competing
beach-level pages and no city page; Google picks one and it converts at 0.56%.

**The natural experiment:** `/nc/kill-devil-hills/kill-devil-hills-kill-devil-hills-nc/water-temp`
is the one page whose beach name *equals* its city name, so it functions as a
city page by accident. It runs **7.71% CTR (2,945 imp, 227 clicks)** against a
family norm of 0.34–0.92%. It earns **227 of the family's 458 clicks — 49.6% of
all water-temp clicks from 7.5% of its impressions.** Position does not explain
it: Ogunquit ranks 6.3 and Smith Point 4.7 with 0.73% and 1.93%.

Mechanism: the page names a different entity than the query asks for. Same root
cause as item 2.

**Caveats, stated plainly.** KDH is n=1 and could be confounded by Outer Banks
seasonality, thinner competition, or brand familiarity. And this traffic is the
*wrong audience*: Plan 064 measured SEO landing conversion at 1 signup per 698
sessions (0.14%), with 74.2% of impressions from water-temp swimmers. This is
simultaneously the largest traffic win available (67,495 combined impressions at
~1%) and the lowest-value audience Quiver reaches. That is a business decision,
not an SEO one, and it should be made deliberately rather than by default.

### 2. `/surf-report/[city]-today` exists for 5 cities; the demand is on 87

`/best-time-to-surf/*` is the largest impression family on the site: **87 pages,
45,152 impressions, 88 clicks, 0.19% CTR**, ranking around position 8.
`/best-time-to-surf/la-jolla` alone draws **25,520 impressions and 9 clicks
(0.04%)**.

`docs/seo/CTR_HISTORY.md` records that La Jolla's GSC query rows are dominated by
`la jolla surf report today` and `la jolla shores surf report today`. The demand
is for a *surf report today*; the page offered is a *best time to surf* page.
There is no `/surf-report/la-jolla-today`.

Where the matching page type does exist it converts far better —
`/surf-report/malibu-today` at 1.96% and `/surf-report/newport-beach-today` at
4.76%, against 0.09% and 0.29% for the same cities' best-time pages.

**Caveats.** Those CTRs rest on 102 and 21 impressions (2 clicks and 1 click) —
far too small to project from. The 2026-08-05 La Jolla title test *failed*: CTR
fell 61.5% even as position improved. And these are curated entries in
`lib/seo/funnel-pages.ts` with bespoke diorama imagery, so this is real
per-page work, not a config loop. Build a handful and measure before scaling.

Unlike item 1, this serves **surfers** — the audience Plan 064 says Quiver is
failing to reach.

### 3. `/cams` and `/surf-cams` are two families competing for one intent

`/surf-cams/san-diego` (1,360 imp, pos 8.7) and `/cams/pacific-northwest`
(637 imp, pos 10.8) sit in separate families with overlapping purpose;
`/cams/east-coast` sits at position 46.2. `tourmaline surf cam` is the only
non-water-temp query in the top 25 (185 imp, pos 5.9). This is consolidation
work, not new content, but it is the third-largest surfer-intent surface.

## Notes

This is a **search-presence proxy**. It measures whether a `quiversurf.app`
result appears in live web search results for each query. It does **not** measure
citations inside Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Gemini, and it is
not an automated Google SERP scrape. No full backlink index or paid backlink API
was used.

Method this run: one live search per query, all 30 canonical queries queried
verbatim, no added qualifiers and no `site:` operators, no retries on a query
that returned nothing, first page only, host matching only `quiversurf.app`. The
query-set 20/10 segment split was validated before the audit.

The product/brand segment moved +20.0 points on a denominator of 10, which is a
two-query move. Do not read it as a 20-point trend; one of the two is a genuine
first appearance and the other (`surfline alternative`) is a return of a query
that had surfaced on 2026-08-18 and 2026-08-19.
