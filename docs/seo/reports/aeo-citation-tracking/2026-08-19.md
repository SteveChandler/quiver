## Notes

This is a **search-presence proxy**. It measures whether a `quiversurf.app`
result appears in live web search results for each query. It does **not**
measure citations inside Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Gemini,
and it is not an automated Google SERP scrape. No paid backlink index was called.

Method this run: one live search per query, all 30 queries, query text verbatim,
no rewording, no `site:` operators, no retries, first page only, host must match
`brandDomains` (`quiversurf.app`). Query set validated at 30 queries in the
20/10 split before running.

## Post-audit investigation (added same day)

The action list above was acted on immediately. Measurement contradicted three of its
five items. The rates in this report stand and the run is **not** voided — only the
action-list diagnosis was wrong. Full detail:
`.planning/2026-08-19-learn-indexation-fix.md`.

**Corrections to the action list:**

1. *"14 near-exact-match pages do not rank"* — misdiagnosed. Titles were already
   near-verbatim matches. GSC URL Inspection showed **11 of 27 `/learn/` pages were not
   indexed at all**: 3 never crawled ("Discovered"), 8 crawled-and-declined. The indexed
   ones sit at avg position 9.7–13.6. `best-tide-for-surfing` (10.2, cited) and
   `best-surf-conditions-for-beginners` (10.0, not cited) differ by ~0.3 positions —
   **this audit's cited/not-cited split is a page-1 threshold effect, not a quality
   signal.** Treating a ±1 query swing as meaningful is reading noise.

2. *beginner-breaks regression* — confirmed and root-caused. Both pages were
   "Discovered - currently not indexed", never crawled, with zero contextual inbound
   links. A separate page, `surf-paddling-for-beginners`, was a **true orphan**: the
   `/learn` hub renders from a hardcoded list that never included it, so it was linked
   from nowhere. Fixed, with a regression test proven to fail on reintroduction.

3. *"[WRITE CONTENT] product/brand category pages"* — **wrong for 1 of 3 targets.**
   `/best-free-surf-forecast-app` and `/best-surf-forecast-app` already exist, are live,
   and are indexed. They are cannibalizing each other: `/best-surf-forecast-app` wins
   "best free surf forecast app" (pos 9.5) while the page built for that query does not
   surface for it. Adding a third page would have made this worse. Only "best app for
   surf session logging" and "personal surf forecast app" genuinely lack pages.

4. *water temperature* — confirmed gap, article written and shipping.

5. *Orange County* — confirmed gap, article written. The stated concern was right for
   the right reason: its two sibling pages fail because they are unindexed, so the new
   page was wired with real inbound links rather than published bare.

**Consequence for future runs:** a query moving in or out of the cited list is not
evidence of anything on its own when the page sits at position ~10. Prefer URL
Inspection coverage state and average position over this audit's binary citation count
when deciding whether to act on an individual query.
