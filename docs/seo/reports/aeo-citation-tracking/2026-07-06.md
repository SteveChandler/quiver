## Notable Gaps

- **Product/brand intent is almost entirely missed.** Quiver is absent for `best surf forecast app`, `best free surf forecast app`, `best surf report app`, `personal surf forecast app`, `best app for surf session logging`, `surf tracker app`, `most accurate surf forecast app`, and `best ai surf forecast app`.
- **LazySurfer dominates product/brand AEO surfaces:** it is the top domain for `best free surf forecast app`, `free surfline alternative`, `personal surf forecast app`, `best app for surf session logging`, and `best ai surf forecast app` (5/10 product queries). This is the single biggest AEO competitor for app-intent queries.
- **Forecast-education queries are uncited despite existing pages.** `how to read a surf forecast`, `how to read a surf report`, `how accurate are surf forecasts`, and `how do machine learning surf forecasts work` return no quiversurf.app result even though `/learn/how-to-read-surf-conditions`, `/forecast-accuracy`, and `/learn/how-quiver-calibrates-your-beach` exist - a citation-readiness/indexing gap, not a content gap.
- **apps.apple.com and surfline.com** hold the top slot for the highest-intent app queries (`best surf forecast app`, `best surf report app`, `most accurate surf forecast app`).
