## Design Context

### Users
Surfers checking conditions before dawn, logging sessions at the beach, sharing with their crew. They open Quiver to decide whether to paddle out, mid-session to log conditions, and post-session to share. The job: make the call confidently and feel part of something.

### Brand Personality
**Chill, Reliable, Smart.** Quiet confidence — like the local who always knows when the swell is hitting. No hype, no corporate polish. Conversational but factual.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: Retro 80s-90s surf culture. Deep Twilight navy (#252D6B) base, Charming Orange (#F78E42) primary accent.
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk (personality headings), DM Sans (data clarity), Space Mono (technical values), Caveat (handwritten accents).
- **Texture**: Sticker aesthetic (1-3deg rotations, asymmetric radius), scan lines, noise overlays. Sparse accents for impact.
- **References**: Stussy/Palace streetwear, Magic Seaweed data-first rawness, local shop zine vibes.
- **Anti-references**: Corporate SaaS (Stripe blue-gray), AI slop (cyan gradients, glassmorphism), Apple minimalism.

### Design Principles
1. **Data is sacred** — Forecast numbers, tide charts, conditions must be crisp and instantly scannable.
2. **Personality over polish** — A slightly rough sticker rotation is better than pixel-perfect corporate alignment.
3. **Sparse accents hit harder** — Use Charming Orange and neon glows sparingly. When everything glows, nothing does.
4. **Surf culture, not surf cliche** — No generic wave illustrations, no "hang loose" clip art. Reference the culture through typography, texture, and attitude.
5. **Respect reduced motion** — All animations must honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Delight Strategy
- **Subtle sophistication** for data pages (micro-interactions, smooth transitions)
- **Playful personality** for achievements and celebrations (confetti, condition-reactive copy)
- **Helpful surprises** for educational content (animated diagrams, visual metaphors)
- **Sensory richness** for key moments (spring physics on toggles, satisfying save animations)

### Animation Toolkit
- Framer Motion (complex), Tailwind keyframes (CSS-only for SEO pages), Canvas Confetti (celebrations)
- Spring curve: `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` for satisfying bounces
- ScrollReveal (fadeUp default) as foundation for all page animations
- GPU optimized: `will-change: transform, opacity` + `translateZ(0)`
